About Us

Finding a suitable room can be difficult, especially for students and working professionals who often have to search street by street. Traditional methods like posters on walls not only take time but also affect the beauty of our city. RoomRental is built to solve this problem by bringing all available rooms onto one simple platform—making the search faster, easier, and more organized.
